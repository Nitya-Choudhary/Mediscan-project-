RESPONSIBLE USE

Helpful information. Not a medical decision-maker.

MediScan is an educational and prescription-literacy interface. OCR can misread handwriting, and AI-generated information can be incomplete or incorrect. It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe medicines, confirm drug interactions or replace a doctor or pharmacist. Do not change a medicine or dosage based on this page. For emergencies, seek immediate medical care.
